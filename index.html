My work sits at the intersection of frontend development, UX/UI design, and design systems . I build web applications and interfaces with HTML, CSS/SCSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, and Vue.js, with particular experience in component-based development, reusable UI patterns, responsive design, and frontend architecture.

I have extensive experience designing and evolving design systems and component libraries, working with UX and engineering teams to turn design patterns into reusable, accessible, and production-ready components. This includes prototyping and interaction design in Figma, documenting systems and components with Storybook, and contributing to their adoption and evolution across products.

My background in graphic and visual design gives me a strong understanding of visual communication, interaction, and brand consistency. Throughout my career I have also worked with web technologies, content-management systems, CI/CD workflows, version control, code reviews, technical documentation, and cross-functional collaboration. More recently, as a Senior Frontend Developer, I have also taken on technical decision-making and mentoring responsibilities.
